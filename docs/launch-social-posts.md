## LinkedIn

My updated website is live: a better home for my writing, work, projects, and talks.

This redesign became a practical exercise in directing AI-assisted development. I set the goals and design direction, reviewed the results, and kept refining the parts that needed judgment—especially the content hierarchy and mobile experience.

The delivery process mattered, too: GitHub feature branches, a separate staging site, CI build checks, and a record of which commit was actually deployed. It gave me room to experiment while keeping production stable.

The new site includes a more useful work history, a browsable writing archive, coding projects, and a recovered collection of presentations, podcasts, interviews, and press. I’ve also added clear disclosures when AI assists with an article.

I wrote a launch post about the process, the challenges, and what’s next. Have a look—and if we’ve worked together, you may spot something familiar.
