## Mentoring

Feature requirements and acceptance criteria: [#54](https://github.com/johnapaz/johnapaz.github.io/issues/54).

| Page | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| My Mentors | Hall of fame for professors, mentors, and influences |
| My Mentees | On-page guestbook form: communication frequency/duration, help received, and a comment |
| Mentorship Articles | Reuse John's related article records |
| Mentorship Resources | Curated resource aggregation |

The guestbook needs durable storage, publication consent, moderation, spam controls, and protected owner review. A static form alone does not complete it. Names, tributes, article choices, and resources must come from John or verified existing content.
