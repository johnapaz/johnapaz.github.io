This website offers optional Google Analytics 4 cookies to understand traffic sources, popular pages, resume downloads, portfolio engagement, and outbound or contact clicks. Contact clicks do not establish that a message was sent.

Analytics starts only when you choose **Allow analytics**. Choose **No thanks** to browse without it. Use **Analytics preferences** on any tracked page to change your choice. Choosing No thanks stops future tracking and removes accessible analytics cookies. Withdrawing consent does not erase data already collected.

Production and staging use separate Google Analytics properties. Local development and the admin area are excluded. Staging data is used to test the integration and does not contribute to production audience reports.

The integration omits URL query strings, fragments, contact addresses, search text, and page titles from its event payloads. It records the page path, referrer origin, broad engagement events, and outbound destination host. Google may process device, browser, and approximate geographic information as part of its analytics service. Advertising personalization is disabled in the site integration.

Your consent preference is stored in your browser separately from analytics cookies. If browser storage is unavailable, analytics remains disabled.

User and event data retention is set to 14 months, with automatic renewal on new user activity disabled. These settings do not govern most aggregated standard reports. Google Signals and enhanced measurement are disabled. Production and staging activation is verified separately.
